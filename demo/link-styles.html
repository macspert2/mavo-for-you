Le sceau, appliqué au texte

The buttons and tiles now wear « sceau frappé » — a tilt on hover, and on press a recoil plus relief. Can a link go the same way? Half of it can, and it is the half that matters.

The motion cannot come along. transform does not apply to a non-replaced inline box at all; the fix is display: inline-block, and the fix is worse than the problem — an inline-block link is one unbreakable box, so a two-word link near the end of a line jumps whole to the next line and the paragraph reflows. In articles that carry a link every few lines, that is fatal. It would also be wrong if it worked: a recoil says "you pressed this", and nobody pressed a word in a sentence.

The relief does come along. text-shadow applies to inline text, never affects layout, and wraps for free — and it is the half readers actually register: the highlight moving from below the glyphs to above them. So the seal's motion belongs to things you press, and its relief to things you mark.

And one constraint nobody expects: white paper cannot be raised. A raised letter needs a highlight lighter than the surface under it, and on #fff there is nothing lighter than the paper — text-shadow: 0 1px 0 white paints white on white. So in article prose the word can only ever sink, never stand proud. Which turns out to be no loss at all: sinking is what a press does, so the one state white paper can express is the only one a link needs.

Deux choses à savoir

Ce que :visited accepte — et refuse

A browser will only honour colour-family properties on :visited: color, background-color, border-*-color, outline-color, column-rule-color, text-decoration-color, text-emphasis-color, and SVG fill / stroke. No pseudo-element content, no opacity, no transform, no shadow, no font change. It also ignores the alpha channel, so an rgba() there renders fully opaque — use a solid colour and mean it. And getComputedStyle() deliberately reports the unvisited values, so script cannot read the state either.

That is why card 11 can only desaturate on visit: its text has no color to change. Anything richer than a colour swap has to be driven by a class, not a selector.

:visited et .mfy-read-link disent deux choses différentes

The plugin already marks links to articles opened during this visit, with .mfy-read-link — a class, session-scoped, gone after 24 hours or a reset. :visited is the browser's own memory: months deep, unreadable, unresettable from the page.

If both are styled, they should agree — pick one visual language, e.g. the muted grey used in proposals A and B, and give it to both. If they disagree, a reader sees two different "already read" signals on the same page and trusts neither. The simplest honest option is to style :visited very lightly (or not at all, as today) and let the session marking carry the meaning.
